# Dice

Every roll is made on the server, so nobody's browser decides a result. The dice tray offers two
kinds of roll.

## Tests

A test is your character trying something under the campaign's rules: pick a characteristic or a
skill and a difficulty, add any other modifiers, and roll. The result card shows the total large,
whether it succeeded, and how the target was reached.

- **Fifth edition:** a D20 Test, d20 plus the ability modifier and the Proficiency Bonus if you're
  proficient, against the difficulty's DC. Choose **Advantage** or **Disadvantage** to roll two d20s
  and keep the higher or the lower; the card shows both dice, the dropped one struck through.
- **A percentile system:** d100 under the target, with degrees of success or failure.

A character with no sheet yet still gets the system's own test, rolled at the rules' human-average
score, and the card says so.

## Free rolls

Below the tests is the dice rack. Tap a die (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100) to add it, tap the
minus beside it to take one away, and set a modifier. A lone d20 can be rolled with Advantage or
Disadvantage. The button shows the formula it will roll, such as `Roll 2d6+3`. For anything the
rack doesn't build, open **Type a formula**: the server reads notation such as `4d6kh3`.

## 3D dice and history

After a roll the dice tumble in 3D and land on the server's values. If your device asks for less
motion, or you turn off **Animate dice**, they appear at rest. The last ten rolls are listed under
the tray, newest first.
