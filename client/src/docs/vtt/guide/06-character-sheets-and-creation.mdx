# Character sheets and creation

A character's sheet is what the rules read: characteristics (ability scores), skills with their
rank (proficient, or Expertise), resources (hit points, Armor Class, Proficiency Bonus), origins
(class, background, species), features, weapons and gear. The sheet view is read-only; changes come
through the creation steps and the GM.

## Building a character

The campaign's pack lists the creation steps. For the fifth-edition SRD:

1. **Choose a class**, then the **origin**: a background and a species. Each choice lists what the
   campaign offers. A player chooses once; the GM may change it.
2. **Ability scores** by one of the pack's methods: the standard array (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8)
   placed as you like, point buy (27 points), or 4d6 keeping the highest three, six times. The
   server rolls, keeps the roll, and checks the scores against the method before writing them.

## NPCs

The GM can give an NPC a sheet from the pack's stat blocks (for 5e, the SRD's humanoids, from
Commoner to Archmage): by naming the block, or from the NPC's role and a line about them, which
picks the closest block. The NPC gets the block's scores, skills, weapons, hit points and Armor
Class, and a Proficiency Bonus by its challenge rating.

## Pregenerated characters

A setting's pregenerated characters are built from their own line, for example "Level 1 Human
Fighter, Acolyte background (SRD 5.2.1)": class, background and species, the standard array as the
SRD's Standard Array by Class places it, the skills and features granted outright, and level-1 hit
points. Every choice the rules leave to the player (skill picks, the background's ability increase,
starting equipment) is reported and left open, never guessed.
