# Tokens

A token is a character (a person in the wiki) or a thing on a scene. A character has one token
across the whole campaign: when they go to another scene, their token leaves the first and appears
on the second, at its entry, and their wiki location follows.

## Ownership

A token's ring colour tells you whose side it is on: yours, friendly, neutral or hostile. You can
move your own characters' tokens; the GM can move any. A token the GM has hidden is drawn faded
for the GM and not at all for players.

## Moving between scenes

The GM moves a character to another scene of the campaign (they leave the docks for the tavern),
places a new character on a scene, or takes a character off a scene when the story only says they
left. Taking them off leaves their wiki location as it was, since the story didn't say where they
went.

## For keyboards and screen readers

A scene can also list its tokens under the map, each with step buttons (north, west, east, south)
for the ones you may move and its name linking to the character's wiki page. The map itself
already takes taps and the keyboard; the list is there for pages that want it.
