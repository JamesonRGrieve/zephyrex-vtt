# Encounters and initiative

An encounter is a fight in a campaign, usually on a scene. The GM starts it with the characters
taking part (by default everyone with a token on the scene). Initiative is rolled under the
campaign's rules pack; a combatant whose sheet has nothing to roll is listed, and the fight waits
until the GM sets their number.

## The order

The combat order shows the round, every combatant from highest initiative down, and whose turn it
is. Ties go to the higher characteristic, then a roll-off. Defeated combatants stay in the list but
are skipped when the turn passes. Players see the order without the GM's hidden combatants.

The GM passes the turn with **Next turn**; after the last combatant a new round begins.

## Attacks

On a combatant's turn, the GM (or rpg-ai acting as GM) chooses among the actions the VTT offers:
the combatant's weapon attacks, from the weapons on their sheet, against any other combatant. An
attack is resolved on the server: the attack roll against the target's Armor Class, a natural 20
a critical hit with the damage dice rolled twice, a natural 1 a miss. The damage comes off the
target's hit points, and at 0 they are defeated.

Each combatant has one action on its turn and one reaction a round, taken off its turn. Distance
isn't checked yet, so every combatant is offered as a target. Spells and spell slots come next.
