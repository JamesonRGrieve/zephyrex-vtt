# Game systems and packs

A campaign plays under a game system. The system's rules pack is where every rule the VTT applies
comes from; nothing about a system's rules is written into the VTT's code.

## The fifth-edition SRD

The public VTT has one system with a pack: **SRD 5.2 (fifth-edition rules)**, `srd-5e`. Its pack is
generated from the SRD's own text, so its numbers and rules are the SRD's as published, under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

## Other systems

A system without a pack still has campaigns, characters and dice formulas, but no tests or
character building. Other systems' rules are installed by their owner on a private instance, as a
content module the campaign then enables, and never published with the VTT.
